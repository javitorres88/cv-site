About

Javier Torres is a Master of Science in Data Science candidate at the CUNY Graduate Center, expected to complete the program in May 2028. He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology with a track in Health Psychology from Hunter College (2024) and an Associate in Arts in Psychology from the Borough of Manhattan Community College (2020).

His research experience, primarily in health psychology, spans Weill Cornell Medicine’s Division of Geriatrics and Palliative Medicine and Hunter College, including work with older adults living with multiple chronic conditions, Hispanic/Latine communities, and survivors of advanced cancer. He completed the Tandon Bridge Program at NYU Tandon (January–July 2025) and is a native Spanish speaker.
